# US-5-1 — Draft location diagram reviewable

## Epic

[E5 — Draft world picture from play (map assist)](../epics/E5-draft-world-picture-map-assist.md)

## User story

**As a** player-explorer documenting the cave
**I want** to request a **draft** structured picture of locations from what I have seen in play
**so that** I can **review**, **copy**, or **discard** it without treating it as authoritative cartography.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

- **WHEN** I ask for a draft location picture **THEN** I **SHALL** receive a **structured diagram** (or equivalent visualization) that I can scan in the ancillary area, **labeled as draft**.
- **WHEN** the draft is shown **THEN** I **SHALL** be able to **dismiss** or hide it without ending my session, closing the shell, or sending commands I did not type—same expectation as hiding other ancillary UI while play continues.
- **WHEN** I copy or export the draft (if offered) **THEN** copied material **SHALL** retain or accompany wording that it is **draft assistance**, not guaranteed game truth.
