# US-4-2 — Operating posture visible

## Epic

[E4 — Session awareness and honest agent surfacing](../epics/E4-session-awareness-and-agent-surfacing.md)

## User story

**As a** visitor using optional assistance
**I want** to see **which assistance posture** is active and **what it will and will not do**
**so that** I am not surprised by how conservative or proactive the assistant behaves.

## Acceptance criteria (EARS)

- **WHEN** I open or focus the assistant area **THEN** I **SHALL** see the **current posture name** in everyday words (for example quick assist, study first, dry run, stabilize—wording may vary if copy stays honest).
- **WHEN** I view that posture **THEN** I **SHALL** see **short plain-language rules**: what kinds of suggestions or previews I should expect, and what the assistant avoids in this posture.
- **IF** posture affects whether drafts can be applied **THEN** I **SHALL** see that distinction **before** any apply action.
